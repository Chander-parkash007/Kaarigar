## 8. 💰 REVENUE STREAMS

### Subscription-Based Model (No Commission!)

| Tier | Price (PKR) | Duration | What They Get | Target Conversion |
|------|-------------|----------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Free** | Rs. 0 | First 2 weeks | Basic profile listing, unlimited | 100% new workers |
| **Boost** | Rs. 50 | 7 days | ⭐ Appear higher in search, Featured badge | 30% of free users |
| **Verified** | Rs. 100 | 30 days | ✅ Top placement, Verified badge, Max trust | 16% of free users |

### Revenue Projection (Conservative)

| Month | Total Workers | Paying Users | Avg. Revenue/User | Monthly Revenue |
|-------|---------------|--------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| 1 | 100 | 0 (free trial) | Rs. 0 | **Rs. 0** |
| 2 | 150 | 25 (17%) | Rs. 500 | **Rs. 12,500** |
| 3 | 200 | 50 (25%) | Rs. 500 | **Rs. 25,000** |
| 6 | 500 | 150 (30%) | Rs. 500 | **Rs. 75,000** |
| 12 | 1,200 | 360 (30%) | Rs. 500 | **Rs. 180,000** |

### Why Subscription > Commission

| Commission Model (InDrive-style) | Subscription Model (Our Choice) |
|----------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| ❌ Worker anxiety about losing money | ✅ Fixed cost, predictable |
| ❌ Requires payment gateway | ✅ Cash payments work fine |
| ❌ Trust issues ("will they cheat me?") | ✅ Transparent pricing upfront |
| ❌ Only earn when transaction happens | ✅ Earn even if worker has slow month |

**Key Insight:** A carpenter earning Rs. 30,000/month paying Rs. 100 = **0.3% of income**. That's less than daily chai expense for consistent client flow.
